# Private production release v5

## Outcome

This patch aligns every story photograph with its associated information card.
It removes the wider negative-offset treatment that could place captions and
credits against or beyond the visible edge, keeps the complete caption inside
the card, and gives long attribution labels safe wrapping at narrow widths.

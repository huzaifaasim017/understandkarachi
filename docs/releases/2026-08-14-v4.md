# Private production release v4

## Outcome

This release makes Understand Karachi a focused, multi-page orientation atlas.
It replaces the decorative world with a progressive 3D Karachi model, keeps
the homepage focused on city-scale understanding, moves traveller preparation
to `/crossings`, and adds a comparison index plus one comparable deep-dive page
for each of the seven reviewed districts.

Roman Urdu remains the first-visit default and English remains selectable on
every route. District, corridor, hub, locality, source, limitation, and safety
content derive from shared canonical records instead of page-specific copies.

## Known limits

- This is a curated orientation atlas, not live navigation, a legal-boundary
  viewer, or a complete road directory. Corridor geometry is schematic and
  changing conditions still require a current source and exact last-mile pin.
- The remote basemap can fail independently. Core relationships, district
  choices, limitations, and onward links remain available in text and local
  data fallbacks.
- Manual browser QA used the connected Chrome environment. Dedicated Firefox,
  WebKit, and assistive-technology sessions were not available for this
  release; semantic, keyboard, locale, fallback, reduced-motion, and responsive
  contracts were additionally checked through automated tests and source
  review. Broader device and participant testing remains roadmap work.
